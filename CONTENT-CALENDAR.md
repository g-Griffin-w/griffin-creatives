## The carousel standard (what "more detail" means)
Every Mon/Wed/Fri carousel has:
1. **Hook slide** (a clear promise, big type).
2. **Why it matters** (one slide, one idea).
3. **3–5 content slides** using a detail layout: `template` (an email in a box), `steps`, `checklist`, `timeline`, `dates`, or `bullets`.
4. **A "mistakes" or "watch out" slide.**
5. **CTA slide** (free guide, link in bio).
Rules: facts come from our own guides or an agency site; dates say which cycle they're from; no invented stats or results; examples labeled "mock."

## Reel format (Tuesday and Thursday)
- **Length:** 15–30 seconds, vertical 9:16. Hook in the first 2 seconds, on-screen text the whole way (most people watch muted).
- **Tuesday, Draw Dates:** the state's dates appear one at a time as text on screen over elk footage or a still from the slide set, ending on the dates graphic. Voiceover or text only.
- **Thursday, Outfitter Question:** the question as big on-screen text over a few seconds of b-roll, or you on camera asking it. No pitch.
- **Every Reel:** same caption rules and the same CTA ("Free guides!" in bio). Post the Reel to the Facebook page too.
- **Production:** shoot or assemble on your phone (Instagram or CapCut). The slide renderer makes stills, not video, so the Reel can reuse its graphics as frames.
- **Tuesday Reel carries the same facts as the old carousel:** verify the dates the week you post.

## Ground rules
- **One CTA, always the same link:** `griffincreativelab.com/starter-kit.html` (no `?lead=`, so signups log as `social`). Bio link carries it.
- **No fake proof.** No client results or testimonials until we have real ones.
- **Don't name-and-shame real outfitters.** Teardowns use mock examples.
- **Verify every date before posting.** Check the agency site the week of posting.
- **Season respect.** Rifle elk is live through November: shorter captions, practical tone. Booking-window content ramps Dec 1.
