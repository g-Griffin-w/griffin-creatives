## Ground rules
- **One CTA, always the same link:** `griffincreativelab.com/starter-kit.html`
  (no `?lead=` param, so signups log as social). Bio link + pinned post carry it.
- **No fake proof.** We have no paying clients or case studies yet. Never post
  invented results, testimonials, or "we helped X". Use the free guides, public
  facts, and our own build-in-public numbers instead. Replace the "proof" slots
  with real client work the day we have it.
- **Don't name-and-shame a real outfitter.** Teardowns use our own mock listing
  or a business that gave permission.
- **Verify every date/number before posting.** Draw dates below were verified
  2026-10-13; recheck on the 1st of each month (agencies move them).
- **Season respect.** Rifle elk is live through November. October/early Nov
  posts stay short and practical; the real booking-window content ramps from
  Dec 1.
