# Testimonial and results kit

Why: every competitor leads with client results or testimonial videos, and we have none yet. Our first 6 founding clients are where our proof comes from. Collect it as you go, not at the end.

## What to collect, and when
| When | What | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Day 0** | A "before" snapshot | Screenshot their Google profile (photos, review count, rating) and note website visits and inquiries per month if they know them |
| **Day 30** | A first check-in | Ask how it's going. If they've had a booking, an inquiry or a nice review, ask for a one-line quote |
| **Day 60** | The testimonial and results permission | A 60–90 second phone video or written quote, plus written OK to share results |
| **Day 90** | The numbers | Compare to the "before": reviews, inquiries, bookings, profile views |

## Rules
- **Only real results, in their words.** Never write a quote for them or round a number up.
- **Numbers need a source:** their booking calendar, Google Business Profile insights, or their own count. Say where it came from ("per the outfitter's booking records").
- **Get permission in writing** (an email reply is enough) before using their name, photos or numbers. Use the wording below.
- **No hunter names or photos of identifiable clients** without that client's permission.
- Don't promise that results will repeat for anyone else.

## The ask (day 60)
> Hi [name], it's been about two months, and I'd really value your honest feedback. Could you record a short video on your phone, about a minute? Just answer a few questions in your own words. No script, and you can say anything, good or bad. If you'd rather write it, a few sentences is fine too. Here are the questions:

## Five questions
1. What was your biggest marketing frustration before we started?
2. What did we set up or change for you?
3. What's different now? (Calls, inquiries, reviews, bookings, anything.)
4. What surprised you?
5. Would you recommend us to another outfitter, and why?

## Phone video tips (for them)
- Hold the phone sideways, in daylight, with camp or the truck behind them. Keep it under 90 seconds. One take is fine.

## Written permission (copy into an email)
> Thanks, [name]. Just to confirm: is it OK if we use your video or quote, your business name, and the results you shared (numbers only, no client names) on our website, social posts and in sales conversations? You can ask us to take it down at any time. Please reply "yes" to confirm.

*Not legal advice. For anything unusual, ask a lawyer.*

## How we use it
- **Homepage and the signup page:** a quote and 2–3 numbers, with the source.
- **Instagram and Facebook:** a 30–45 second cut of the video as a Reel, plus a carousel: "Before, what we did, after."
- **Sales calls:** one sentence, "We helped [business] with [result]," only when true.
- **Case study page:** a one-page write-up, with their permission.
