## Mon Nov 2 · Rebook Monday: six ways to open the thank-you email (9 slides)
Slides: `2026-11-02-email-1-openers/slide-01.png` to `slide-09.png`.

**Caption**
> Season's wrapping up, so this week's email is the thank-you. Don't open it with "thank you for choosing us." Open it with one real moment from the hunt.
>
> Fill in the blank: "Thanks for hunting with us. I keep coming back to ___."
> 1. The bull: "that bull at first light on day four"
> 2. The hard day: "the long pack out. You never complained once."
> 3. The sound: "the night the bugling wouldn't quit"
> 4. The weather: "that morning the snow came in sideways and we kept going"
> 5. Who they came with: "watching you and your dad glass that basin together"
> 6. The quiet moment: "those ten minutes on the ridge when nobody said a word"
>
> No ask in this one. Send it within a week. Not sure of the moment? Ask your guide.
