# Tue Nov 3: Draw Dates Tuesday Reel: Idaho ("If I guided in Idaho…")

About 35 seconds, vertical 9:16. Stills in `2026-11-03-idaho-reel/` (6 frames); use them as the Reel's frames, about 5 to 6 seconds each, or record yourself over them. Captions on the whole way.

**Dates checked Nov 3 against Idaho Fish and Game (idfg.idaho.gov):** fall 2026 controlled hunt applications May 1 to June 5; first draw results posted June 17, 2026; drawn tags had to be claimed by Aug 1; second draw applications Aug 5 to 15, results Aug 20; leftover tags first-come from Aug 25. 2027 dates are not posted yet, so the Reel says these are 2026 dates.
**Timing choice:** the strategy doc said "before May 1". I used mid-May instead (2 to 3 weeks before June 5), which matches our Idaho dates page and the guide's Email 3.
