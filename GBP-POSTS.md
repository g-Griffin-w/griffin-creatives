## How posts work
In the profile (search "Griffin Creative Labs" while signed in, then **Posts**, then **Add update**): one photo, up to about 1,500 characters of text (keep it to 2 to 3 sentences), and an optional button. Use **Learn more**. Updates stay on the profile; newer ones show first. Post one a week (Fridays) so the profile never looks dead. Do not use the Instagram carousel slides: they have "swipe" and page numbers.

The first post we published (Nov 2) says "four free guides". It is out of date; the posts below say five.

Button link for every post (change only the last word):
`https://www.griffincreativelab.com/starter-kit.html?utm_source=google&utm_medium=gbp-post&utm_campaign=` + the post name below.
