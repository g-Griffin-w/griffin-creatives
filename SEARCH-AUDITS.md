# Search audits for calls (2 minutes per prospect)

BLUEPRINTS.md #3, lead-magnet mode. The point is an opener that is about *their* business and true, not a generic pitch.

## How to run one
1. `node scripts/search-audit.js <their website>` prints what Google can read from the home page: title and description, headings, schema, image alt text, phone as text, and the pages in the menu.
2. On your phone, open Google Maps and search `[business name] [town]`. Does a listing exist? How many reviews? Photos? Is the phone number the same as on the site? (I can't see Maps from here, so this one is yours.)
3. Search `elk hunting outfitter [their region]` and note whether they appear and what the top three titles say.
4. Pick **three** gaps that are really there. Say what is good first, then the gaps. Never say a site is bad when the basics are fine.

## 1. Utah Big Game Outfitters (call Sat Oct 31, noon Mountain)
(435) 749-1091 · utahbiggameoutfitters.com · Green River, UT · co-owners Coby Hunt, Devin LeRoy, JT Robbins (per the site)

**What is already good (say this first):** a real WordPress site with an SEO plugin (All in One SEO), a description and canonical on every page, social preview tags, schema, alt text on every image, a separate page for each species, and the phone number as clickable text. They know the basics.

**The three gaps:**
1. **Only 3 Google reviews, and the newest is about 7 years old** (seen on Maps Oct 31). Their profile has successful-hunt photos and a few videos of big game, so the listing is set up well; it just looks dormant. A hunter comparing outfitters sees 3 old reviews and wonders whether they still operate. This is the lead gap and the quickest fix: a review request after every hunt.
2. **No reviews, prices or dates on the site.** The home page never mentions a review, a price, or 2027, and there is no link to the Google profile to send hunters there.
3. **The elk unit pages have no real address.** La Sal, Manti and Book Cliffs elk are `?page_id=924`, `929` and `936`, so they are hard to share and tell Google nothing. These are the pages people search for.

Smaller: no social links except YouTube. The title is 96 characters and starts with "Mountain Lion Hunting Utah", so Google cuts it off and the first words are about lion, not elk.

**Opener (the goal is a 15-minute call about us running it, not a guide):** "I looked at your site and your Google page before I called. The photos and videos are great, and the site is solid. The one thing hurting you is that you have 3 reviews and the newest is from 7 years ago, so a hunter comparing outfitters thinks you stopped booking. That's the kind of thing we run for outfitters: we tune your Google page, give you a simple way to ask every hunter for a review and reply to them for you, and email your past hunters on the right dates so they rebook. Do you have 15 minutes this week for me to show you what that would look like for you?"

If they say "send me something," send the audit points and the Calendly link first, and the guides second. The guides prove we know the work; they are not the pitch.

## 2. 2 H Outdoors LLC (the one real signup)
(406) 596-9821 (also (406) 596-9819 on the directory) · 2houtdoors.com · listed under Sheridan, MT / "outside of Dillon" · **owner: Charles McDougal** (MOGA directory, license 26388)

**Correction:** the signup came in under the name "Colton Barness" from a personal email, not the business address. The owner on the MOGA listing is Charles McDougal, so that signup is a family member or staff, not the owner. Ask for Charles and say someone there asked for the guides.

**What is already good:** listed on MOGA, SouthwestMT, VisitMT and Guidefitter. A pricing page, FAQ and lodging page. A clear 5-day, eight-hunters-a-week pitch. Clickable phone number.

**The three gaps:**
1. **Thin, unfinished site.** About 265 words on the home page, a GoDaddy website builder page with two identical H1 headings, no canonical tag, no schema, and none of its 3 images have alt text.
2. **No reviews anywhere on the site, no 2027 dates,** and no link to Google reviews or social pages.
3. **The listings disagree.** MOGA says Sheridan, the site's description says outside Dillon, and two phone numbers are in use. Google trusts a business more when its name, location and number match everywhere. They do have a Maps listing (checked Oct 31); compare its address and phone to the site and MOGA.

**Opener (the goal is a 15-minute call about us running it):** "Someone at 2 H signed up for our free marketing guides, so I wanted to check they came through. I also looked at how you show up on Google. You're on the right directories, but your site, MOGA and Google don't agree on where you are, and there are no reviews on the site. That's what we fix for outfitters: one consistent Google page, review requests after every hunt, and rebooking emails to past hunters. Do you have 15 minutes this week for me to show you what that looks like for 2 H?"
