## Webinar outline (30 min + Q&A)

**Title:** Fill your 2027 cancellations: four free marketing systems for outfitters

1. **The booking window gap (3 min).** Hunt season is loud; next year's
   bookings happen quietly in winter.
2. **System 1 — Google Business Profile (7 min).** Categories, service area,
   photos, Q&A. Live walkthrough.
3. **System 2 — Review requests (6 min).** The one-text message and when to
   send it.
4. **System 3 — Rebooking emails (6 min).** The three-email sequence, word for
   word.
5. **System 4 — The draw-season calendar (4 min).** What to send before
   applications open, by state.
6. **How to get the guides (1 min).** starter-kit.html. No pitch beyond that.
7. **Q&A (remaining time).**

## Rules for ourselves

- **No fake credentials.** We have no case studies yet; don't claim client
  results. Lead with "free and practical."
- **Be honest about who we are.** Small team, elk outfitters only, no
  contracts.
- **Don't send mass mail.** Associations are a handful of conversations,
  personalized one at a time.
- **Offer, don't push.** If they decline the webinar, ask for the newsletter
  mention instead.
- **Before sending:** confirm each association's contact and event dates, and
  that the guides' state calendar dates are still current.
