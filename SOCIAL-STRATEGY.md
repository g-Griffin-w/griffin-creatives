# Social strategy reset (Fri Oct 30, 2026)

## What's wrong with what we've posted
- **It names problems and teaches nothing.** "5 questions a hunter asks" and "where do your bookings come from?" leave the outfitter knowing what we think but not able to do anything.
- **It's text over skull stills.** No person, no screen, no proof anything is real. Outfitters buy from people.
- **We ask before we give.** Every post ends "free guides in bio" without the viewer having gotten anything first.
- **It talks about our guides, not their problem.** Their problem is an empty 2027 calendar.

## The fix: one Reel format, "If I was an outfitter…"
Every Reel does one specific thing an outfitter can copy in 10 minutes or less.

**The rules**
1. **Hook in the first 2 seconds:** "If I was an outfitter, here's how I'd ___ ."
2. **One action per Reel.** Show it on screen (a phone screen recording, an email, a spreadsheet, a Google listing), not just say it.
3. **Give the real thing.** The exact words of the email or message, the exact bio, the exact columns. Put the copy-paste text in the caption too, so the caption alone is worth saving.
4. **You on camera or your voice over a screen recording.** Captions on the whole way (most people watch muted). No stock footage, no skull stills as the main visual.
5. **Honest framing.** "If I was an outfitter" is true and avoids fake results. No client results until we have real ones. Examples are labeled "mock."
6. **CTA is a comment, not a bio link.** "Comment 2027 and I'll send you the checklist." Every comment is a warm lead: reply, DM the checklist and starter-kit link, then call.
7. **Length 30–45 seconds.** Post to Facebook too.

## What to stop
- Text-only Reels over skull stills.
- "Question" Reels with no answer from us. Ask the question and give our answer in the same Reel.
- Ending on "free guides in bio." The comment keyword replaces it; the bio link stays as backup.

## What to measure (weekly)
| Metric | Healthy sign |
|---|---|
| Comments with the keyword | 3+ per Reel by week 3 |
| Saves and shares | Higher than the old carousels |
| DMs from outfitters | Any. Reply same day, then call |
| Follows from outfitter accounts | 5+ per week by week 6 |
If a format gets nothing after 4 posts, change the hook, not the idea.
