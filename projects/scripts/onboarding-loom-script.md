# Onboarding Loom Script — Record Once, Reuse Forever

**Target length:** 5-7 minutes
**Tone:** Casual, supportive, direct. Like a smart friend.
**Setup:** Open Loom Chrome extension. Camera + screen share. Have a sample client Drive folder open on screen.

---

Open your action plan. Block your Friday. Let's get you some customers. Talk soon."

## RECORDING NOTES

- **Background:** Clean. Sit near a window for natural light if possible.
- **Audio:** Use a USB mic if you have one. Built-in laptop mic is OK but airpods are noticeably worse for cold prospects.
- **Energy:** Look at the camera when on camera. Smile in first 5 seconds. Don't read the script — talk it.
- **Length target:** 5-7 minutes. Under 5 feels rushed, over 8 loses attention.
- **Filler words:** First take is allowed to have "ums" — don't re-record. Authenticity beats polish.

## HOW TO USE THE LOOM AFTER RECORDING

1. Save the Loom (don't make it public unless you want random people seeing it)
2. Copy the share link
3. Add to your **delivery email template** in Make.com Scenario C Gmail modules. Place it ABOVE the Drive folder button:
   - "👋 START HERE — Watch this 5-minute walkthrough first: [LOOM LINK]"
4. Add to your **welcome email** in Scenario B (the one sent right after Stripe payment)
5. Optionally embed on your website's "Customer Onboarding" page later
6. Update the Loom every 6 months as you learn what new clients struggle with
