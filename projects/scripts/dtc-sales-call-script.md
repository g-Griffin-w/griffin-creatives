# DTC Sales Call Script — 15 min Calendly Discovery

**Goal:** Diagnose creative-volume pain, validate fit, soft-pitch one tier (don't push close on first call), book follow-up if needed.

**Energy:** Peer-to-peer, no pitching. You are a creative operator talking to a founder/CMO who already runs ads. Speak the language. Talk ROAS, hook rate, CPM, CAC, blended.

**Pre-call (5 min):**
1. Open their Shopify (Wappalyzer if you don't know)
2. Open Meta Ad Library — search their brand name. Note ad count, formats, freshness.
3. Open their site → 1 product page. Note copy quality, social proof, urgency mechanics.
4. Note 2-3 specific things you'd say if asked "what would you do differently."

---

## OPENER (0:00 - 1:00) — Set the tone, kill awkward silence

> "Hey [first name], Gabriel here. Thanks for booking. Before I forget — I poked around [brand name] before this call. Big fan of [specific thing — a hook in their current ad, a product, their about page positioning, whatever's actually good]. Quick way I like to run these calls: I'll ask you 4-5 questions about how you're running creative right now, you'll ask whatever you want, and we'll figure out if there's a fit. Sound good?"

**Why:** Specific compliment proves you did homework. "How you're running creative" sets the agenda. "If there's a fit" frames this as mutual qualification — kills the high-pressure-sales smell.

---

## DISCOVERY (1:00 - 8:00) — Listen 80% of the time

### Q1: Pipeline of creative

> "Walk me through how you're producing creative right now. In-house, agency, UGC marketplace, or some mix?"

**Listen for:** Is there a bottleneck? Are they paying $5K+/mo to an agency? Do they have a media buyer waiting on creative? Are they personally writing scripts at midnight?

### Q2: Hook rate / fatigue

> "What's your current hook rate on Meta cold, roughly? And how often are you having to swap creative before fatigue sets in?"

**Listen for:** If they say "hook rate? what?" → less sophisticated buyer, need to educate gently. If they say "we're at 35% on winners but they burn in 14 days" → sophisticated buyer, you're golden, talk numbers.

### Q3: Volume vs. quality tension

> "If I waved a wand and gave you 20 fresh static ads + 10 UGC scripts a week, would your media buyer use them or would they sit in a Drive folder?"

**Listen for:** Are they CAPABLE of deploying volume? If they don't have a media buyer or in-house creator → they need a managed-service, not us. (Disqualify gracefully.)

### Q4: Spend + ROAS context

> "Ballpark monthly ad spend right now? And what ROAS would make a $1,750-3,500/mo creative cost feel like a no-brainer?"

**Listen for:** If <$5K/mo spend → too early for us, recommend they revisit when they're at $15K+/mo. If $15K-200K/mo → strong fit. If $200K+/mo → they probably need a real performance agency, but we can still serve as a creative-supplement vendor.

### Q5: What hasn't worked

> "What have you tried that didn't work — agencies, in-house creators, UGC marketplaces, anything? What broke?"

**Listen for:** This is the gold. They'll tell you exactly what their objection to working with you will be before you even pitch. Solve it in your soft pitch.

---

## SOFT PITCH (8:00 - 12:00) — Reflect back, then propose

> "OK so here's what I'm hearing: [paraphrase their pain in 2 sentences]. The way we'd solve that is [explain the deliverable mix that maps to their pain — don't recite the whole tier]. The tier that fits you is [Launch/Scale/Dominate] at [$X/mo]. We deliver everything in 48 hours, no contracts, month-to-month. Couple things to know:
> - We don't deliver finished UGC video — we deliver the brief. Your creators or paid UGC actors film it. We do this because AI UGC kills conversion.
> - First month is the test month. If creative isn't lifting CTR or ROAS by week 3, cancel and we part friends.
> - Quick reality check before I send a checkout link — does this sound like what you'd actually deploy, or am I missing what you really need?"

**Why each part matters:**
- Paraphrase pain → proves you listened
- Map deliverable to pain → not feature-dumping
- Honest about UGC limit → builds trust, kills future surprise
- "Month-to-month, no contracts, cancel anytime" → kills the #1 DTC founder objection
- Final question → invites pushback so you address it BEFORE the close

---

## CLOSE OR PUNT (12:00 - 15:00)

### IF they say "yes, makes sense":
> "Cool — I'll send the [Tier] checkout link in chat. Pay whenever you're ready. Within 5 minutes of payment you'll get an onboarding form (5 questions, takes 3 minutes). Within 48 hours of submitting that, you'll have your full creative package in your Google Drive. Cool?"

Send Stripe link in Calendly chat or follow-up email IMMEDIATELY after call. Don't wait.

### IF they say "I need to think":
> "Totally. What specifically? Pricing, fit, timing, or want to see a sample of what we'd actually deliver?"

**Most common answers + your response:**
- "Pricing" → "Got it. We can start at the Launch tier ($700) to test the model, upgrade in month 2 if you want more volume."
- "Want to see samples" → "Easy. I'll send you a 5-min Loom showing one of our recent client's actual deliverables. Open it tonight, reply if it's a fit."
- "Need to talk to my [partner / CFO / media buyer]" → "Makes sense. Want me to send a 1-page brief you can forward? Saves you having to repeat the pitch."

### IF they're not a fit (low spend, no media buyer, wrong stage):
> "Honestly? You're probably 6 months early. Until you're spending $10K+/mo on Meta with someone deploying creative daily, hiring us would burn cash that's better spent on inventory. Stay in touch — when you cross that threshold, come back."

**Why this matters:** Saying "you're not a fit" makes the YES from the next caller more believable. It's also true. Don't sell to people who'll churn in 30 days.

## NOTES TO SELF

- Mute notifications. Be present.
- If they go silent on a question, count to 5 before re-asking. Silence pulls answers out of people.
- Don't trash-talk competitors (Billo, agencies, ChatGPT). Acknowledge them as part of the stack.
- If they mention a competitor brand by name, look it up immediately — bonus points if you reference back their competitor's creative.
- Take notes during the call. Specifically: their numbers (spend, ROAS, hook rate), their pain language (use it verbatim in the follow-up email).
- After 5 calls, audit which questions actually drove the close vs which were filler. Cut the filler.

**Target metrics:**
- Show-up rate: 60%+ (Calendly reminders help)
- Pitch-to-close on call: 25-35% (DTC founders move fast when they trust you)
- Pitch-to-close within 7 days (including delayed-yes): 40-55%
- Average deal size: target Scale tier ($1,750) as the default — most DTC brands should be there
