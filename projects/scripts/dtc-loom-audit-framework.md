# DTC Loom Audit Framework — 5 Minute Video Template

**When to use:** Cold email reply "yes please send the audit" → record this Loom within 24 hours → send back as reply with Calendly link in PS.

**Goal:** Demonstrate value in 5 minutes. Give them 2-3 specific creative ideas they could implement TOMORROW (even without hiring us). The audit IS the pitch.

**Tools needed before recording:**
1. Their website (1 product page open)
2. Meta Ad Library (https://www.facebook.com/ads/library) — search their brand name
3. Their TikTok page (if they have one)
4. Your face on camera + screen share enabled in Loom
5. Quiet room, decent lighting

---

## RECORDING STRUCTURE (5:00 total)

### Section 1 — Opener (0:00 - 0:30) — Camera only, no screen share

> "Hey [first name], Gabriel here from GriffinCreative. You said 'send the audit', so here we go. Real quick — this is 5 minutes, screen-share style, 3 specific things I'd change about your creative based on what I'm seeing. No pitch baked in. If it's useful, awesome, if not, no hard feelings. Let's go."

**Why:** Sets expectation. "No pitch baked in" disarms. Specific length sets boundary so they actually watch.

---

### Section 2 — Their site (0:30 - 1:30) — Screen share their homepage + 1 product page

Show their site. Talk through:

1. **First impression (5 sec):** "Within 5 seconds of landing on your site, can a stranger tell me what you sell, who it's for, and why I'd want it? Let's see..." → Read aloud what stands out. Be honest. If it's confusing, say so.

2. **Hero section diagnostic:** Headline doing the work? CTA clear? Above-the-fold credibility (press logos, review count, "as seen in")?

3. **Product page diagnostic:** Hero image authentic or stock-y? Price visible without scroll? Social proof above the fold? Urgency mechanism (low stock, limited time, free shipping threshold)?

**Output:** 1 specific change you'd make. Just 1 — don't dump 10.

Example: "Your hero says 'Premium [category] for the modern [audience]' which is true of literally every brand in your space. If we swapped that for something like '[Specific outcome customers actually get]' — way more scroll-stopping. Look at how [a competitor or admired brand] does this..."

---

### Section 3 — Their Meta ads (1:30 - 3:00) — Screen share Meta Ad Library

Open https://www.facebook.com/ads/library, search their brand. Show what's running.

1. **Volume diagnostic:** How many ads currently active? Last new ad date? If <5 active or last new ad >14 days old → "you're sitting on creative fatigue right now."

2. **Format mix:** Mostly static? Mostly video? UGC vs polished? Diversity matters.

3. **Hook diagnostic:** Read 2-3 of their first 3 seconds out loud. Are they hooks or are they "[Brand Name] presents..."? The latter is a thumbstop killer.

**Output:** 1 specific hook angle they're missing. Example: "I see you have 6 product-shot statics live, all with the same generic headline. You're missing a 'curiosity hook' format — something like '[contrarian statement about their category]'. That format alone usually adds 20-40% lift on cold."

---

### Section 4 — UGC / organic social (3:00 - 4:00) — Screen share their TikTok/IG

Open their TikTok or IG Reels.

1. **Post cadence:** Posting daily? Weekly? Dead account?
2. **Format authenticity:** Are they posting real-human UGC or repurposed ad creative? UGC-feel wins on these platforms.
3. **What's working for them:** Note any post with significantly higher views than average. "This one got 47K views vs your average 800 — that's your hook. Why aren't you running this as a paid ad?"

**Output:** 1 specific thing — either "post more UGC like this" or "you have a viral format right here, turn it into a paid ad."

---

### Section 5 — The close (4:00 - 5:00) — Camera only, no screen share

> "OK that's the audit. Three things to recap: (1) [hero copy change], (2) [missing hook angle], (3) [UGC opportunity]. Try those this week — even if you don't work with us, those should move numbers.
>
> If you DO want help: we ship 20-50 fresh ad concepts a month, ready-to-deploy, $700-3,500 depending on volume, no contracts. Calendly link below if you want to talk for 15 minutes. Otherwise, hope this was useful. Talk soon."

**Why:** Soft close. You named 3 free fixes (high reciprocity). Pricing range in one breath. Calendly is opt-in. No high-pressure. They're way more likely to convert because you over-delivered before asking.

## POST-RECORDING CHECKLIST

After clicking "stop":
1. **Title the Loom:** `[Brand name] creative audit — Gabriel @ GriffinCreative`
2. **Add Calendly link to the Loom's description**
3. **Reply to their cold email response** with:

4. **Set a 48-hour reminder** to follow up if no reply.

---

## QUALITY BAR — KILL CRITERIA

If after recording you'd say YES to any of these → re-record:
- Did I just describe the audit instead of doing it?
- Did I name 0 specific things to change?
- Did I pitch in the first 2 minutes?
- Did I exceed 6 minutes?
- Was my energy flat?

A great audit gets opened, watched to 80%+, and converts ~30-40% to a booked call. A mediocre audit gets skimmed and forgotten.

---

## CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT

Track for each audit you record:
- Brand name + audit date
- Did they reply within 7 days?
- Did they book a call?
- Did they buy?

After 10 audits, audit your audits. Which sections drove engagement? Which were skipped? Cut what isn't working.
