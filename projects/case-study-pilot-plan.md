# Case-Study Pilot Plan — Manufacturing GriffinCreative's First Proof

**Goal:** Produce ONE publishable, credible result that proves the system works on a real DTC brand. This is the single highest-leverage asset for converting cold prospects. Everything else (DMs, copy, guarantees) converts better once this exists.

**Definition of done:** A "Teardown 002" style proof page on griffincreativelab.com showing: a real brand, the creative we shipped, a before/after metric, and a 2-line quote from the operator. Permission to publish secured in writing.

---

## 1. The honest claim (read this first)

We do **not** run ads or manage campaigns. That means we cannot honestly claim "we 3x'd their revenue" — we didn't control spend, budget, or targeting. Claiming outcomes we didn't drive is the fastest way to get torched by a sharp buyer on a call.

What we CAN claim, cleanly and defensibly, is **creative-level performance**: our creative beat the brand's existing control on the metrics creative actually drives —

- Hook rate / thumbstop (3-sec view %)
- CTR (link click-through)
- CPC / CPM
- Cost per add-to-cart or CPA at the ad level

The case study framing is therefore: *"Our finished creative outperformed [Brand]'s existing best ad on CTR by X% / dropped CPA by Y% in a 14-day head-to-head test."* That is true, attributable to us, and exactly what a media buyer cares about.

---

## 2. Who to pick (pilot ICP)

The pilot brand is NOT just any DTC brand — it must be able to produce a clean result fast. Hard filters:

- **Already spending on paid ads** ($5k–$50k/mo sweet spot). No spend = no data = no case study.
- **Single decision-maker** (founder or solo marketer). Committees stall.
- **Will actually deploy.** The #1 risk is a brand that takes free creative and never runs it. Screen for someone hungry and responsive.
- **Clean product photos available** so our output looks its best.
- **A category we can make look good** — visually interesting product (beverage, beauty, apparel, supplements, home goods).

Where to source the pilot, in priority order:
1. A **warm email responder already in your pipeline** — someone who replied but hasn't bought. The free pilot is the perfect re-engagement offer.
2. Your **personal/founder network** — anyone running a Shopify store doing real ad spend.
3. A **micro-influencer-led brand** — often hungry for creative and fast to deploy.

---

## 3. The offer (what gets a yes)

Keep it lopsided in their favor. The script:

> "I'll build you a full month of finished ad creative — designed statics, captioned video ads, hooks — completely free. No catch. All I ask in return: run at least 5 of them against your current best ad with a small test budget over 2 weeks, and let me publish the results plus a couple lines from you on how it went. If the creative doesn't beat your control, you've lost nothing."

Why this works: total risk reversal for them, and the "let me publish the results" ask is the entire point of the pilot. Get a **written yes** to publishing (even a one-line email/DM reply is fine) BEFORE you start.

---

## 4. Capture the baseline (do this before shipping anything)

You can only show "before/after" if you record the "before." On the kickoff call, get:

- Their **current best-performing ad** + its CTR / CPA / hook rate (screenshot from Ads Manager).
- Their typical **account-average CTR / CPA** as the control benchmark.
- Agreement on **which metric is the headline** (usually CTR or ad-level CPA).
- The **test budget and window** (e.g., $50/day split across 5 creatives for 14 days).

No baseline = no credible case study. This step is non-negotiable.

---

## 5. Timeline (~4–5 weeks start to published)

**Week 0 — Lock the pilot.** Secure the brand, get the publish-permission yes, capture baseline, run onboarding brief.

**Week 1 — Ship the batch.** Deliver the full finished creative set in 48h (your normal flow). Hand them a dead-simple deployment instruction: "run these 5 against your control, $X/day, don't touch it for 14 days."

**Weeks 2–3 — Test runs.** Let it cook. Light-touch check-ins (you're not managing — just making sure they actually launched). Pull mid-point numbers at day 7.

**Week 4 — Collect results + quote.** Get final screenshots, calculate the head-to-head lift, grab a 2-line quote on a quick call (record it — quotes are easier spoken than typed).

**Week 5 — Package and publish.** Build "Teardown 002 — [Brand], Real Results" using your existing teardown format. Push live. Now every outreach links to it.

---

## 6. How to package it into proof

Mirror your Liquid Death teardown layout, but swap hypothetical for real:

- The brand + product (logo with permission, or anonymized "a $2M beverage brand" if they're shy).
- The control ad vs our winning ad, side by side.
- The headline metric: "+47% CTR vs control" / "-31% CPA in 14 days" (use real numbers).
- The 2-line operator quote.
- A soft CTA: "Want the same teardown for your brand? Reply and we'll build it."

Anonymized-but-real still beats hypothetical. Don't let "they won't let me use their name" stop you — "a DTC supplement brand spending $20k/mo" with real screenshots is plenty credible.

---

## 7. Risks & mitigations

- **They never deploy.** Biggest risk. Mitigate: pick a hungry, responsive operator; make deployment idiot-proof; agree on the test window up front.
- **The creative loses.** Possible. Mitigate: run enough variants (5+) that at least one beats control; if the whole batch loses, that's a product signal worth more than any case study — listen to it. Don't fabricate a win.
- **No clean baseline.** Mitigate: refuse to start until you've screenshotted their control metrics.
- **Permission walk-back.** Mitigate: get the publish yes in writing before you spend effort.

---

## 8. Fallback: the house-brand test

If you genuinely can't get a brand to commit in ~2 weeks, run the system on a brand **you control** — a small Shopify product (even a cheap dropship/print-on-demand SKU you spin up) — and spend $300–500 of your own on a controlled A/B: our creative vs a generic baseline ad.

Less credible (it's your own product) but fully in your control and still produces real Ads Manager screenshots and a real CTR/CPA comparison. Frame it honestly as "we tested our own system" — a transparent self-test still beats an empty proof section.

This is the backup, not plan A. A real third-party brand is worth 5x a house test for conversion. Exhaust option 3.1 (warm responders) hard before falling back.

---

## Bottom line

Don't add more deliverables — you ship finished creative already. The thing standing between you and signed clients is **one real result on the site.** Run one lopsided free pilot, capture the baseline, prove a creative-level win, publish it as Teardown 002. That asset does more for conversion than any DM or pricing tweak.
