# Marketing strategies — everything in one pile (Oct 19, 2026)

Goal: first 3 paying elk-outfitter clients (Booked Out, $2,800/mo), then a repeatable way to get more.
Constraints: one person, about 6 hours a day, no case studies yet, and outfitters are in camp until December. The real buying window is **Dec–Mar**.

Ratings: **Effort** L/M/H · **Cost** $0 / $ / $$ · **Speed** = how soon it could produce a real conversation (Fast = days, Med = weeks, Slow = months).
**Status:** ON = running now · READY = built, not launched · TODO = not started. I have not tested any of these on outfitters, so the ratings are my judgment.

## The pile, ranked (what I'd do first)
1. **Phone calls to warm and phone-only leads** (fastest path to a conversation).
2. **Founding-client offer**, pitched on those calls.
3. **Free Google-profile audit offer** on every call and reply.
4. **Association calls** for one introduction each.
5. **IOGA vendor table (Dec 1)** and the Wyoming Feb 2 deadline push in December.
6. **Instagram + Facebook** (keep posting; engage 20 outfitter accounts a day).
7. **Personalized audit videos** to the 10 best-fit leads.
8. **Booking agents**, softly, once an outfitter names one.
9. **Boosted-post test** with a small budget, to see who responds.
10. **Cold email**: keep it running at 23/day, not more.
11. **Direct mail** in December to the best 50–100 leads.
12. **SEO, free tools and newsletter**: build slowly in the background.

## Weekly hours (a 6-hour day)
- 90 min phone · 45 min posting and engagement · 45 min email and inbox · 60 min lead-building · 60 min relationship work (associations, agents, supplier partners) · 30 min wrap-up and logging.

## What not to do
- Don't buy email lists or scrape personal data. Our leads come from public business registries.
- Don't text cold or send mass DMs.
- Don't claim client results we don't have.
- Don't spend on ads before we know the message works.

## Open decisions
- The founding-client price and number of spots.
- Whether to test a small paid boost.
- Whether to widen beyond NW + WY (Colorado and Utah).
- Whether to book the IOGA vendor table (Dec 1).

## What to measure
Calls made, emails collected, conversations booked, founding-client yeses, signups from social, and replies. Review weekly. Cut anything with zero response after 4 weeks.
