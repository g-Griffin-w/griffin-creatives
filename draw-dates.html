WHEN TO EMAIL YOUR CLIENTS

A reminder lands best two to three weeks before the deadline. For Wyoming nonresidents that means December or early January. For Montana and Colorado, mid-March. For Utah, early April. For Oregon, mid-April. For California, mid-May. The reminder helps them whether they book with you or not, and the ones who apply remember who reminded them.
